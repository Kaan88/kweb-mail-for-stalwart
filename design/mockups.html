Scope against Stalwart 1.1

Every row is something Stalwart 1.1.0 advertises over JMAP. Phase 1 is the app you can use daily; later phases add to it without changing its structure.

How it is put together

Rust coreJMAP sync, the local cache, sending, and the rule that decides which notifications should exist. Shared with nothing on screen, tested on the desktop.
Kotlin shellJetpack Compose screens, the connection service, Android notifications and their actions. It asks the core what to show and never talks to the server itself.
The bridgeUniFFI generates the Kotlin bindings from the Rust interface, so the two sides cannot drift apart.
