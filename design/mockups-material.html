kweb mail in Material You, second pass

A review of the first Material 3 Expressive pages, and the same direction carried through the reading milestone (M3) as it is built today. Twelve screens. Switch the colour source and light or dark below; every screen says where it stands.

What the review found

  • Read mail had no avatar colour. The avatar used two colour roles that were never defined, so the circle of a read message was invisible. It now uses the secondary container.
  • The unread badge was white on pale red in dark mode. It now takes the on-error colour.
  • Two layout slips. The sender avatar on the message screen stretched into a wide pill, and list rows squeezed each other when the list was full. Both are fixed.
  • The seven screens predate M3. There was no conversation, no folder list, no search and no notice for hidden images, all of which exist in the app now. They are drawn here.
  • Delivery check only showed the good day. Every status pill had the same colour, so a failure had no way to look different. A failing state is added.
  • Three home tiles could not be built. "To pay", "Trip" and "People" need the app to understand what a mail says; nothing before the AI assistant (M7) does that. The overview here uses only data the app has.
  • The watch is left out. A Wear OS app is not in the plan, and a paired watch already shows the phone's notifications with their Archive and Mark read actions. It is yours to add to the plan or not.
  • The notification shade is Android's. The app chooses icon, texts, actions and accent colour; the card shapes in that picture are the system's and will differ by phone.

For the build

Colour is one fileThe app already uses Material 3 components and takes every colour from the theme; only the theme file holds the crimson. Dynamic colour is a change there: the wallpaper palette on Android 12 and later, a scheme seeded from the Ward crimson below that (the app supports Android 10).
Shapes need no new libraryGrouped lists with large outer and small inner corners, and square or round avatars, are plain shapes. The connected button group, the floating toolbar and the morphing icon are newer Material pieces; whether the pinned library version has them as stable needs checking before they are promised.
Status coloursThe app has its own green and amber for "ok" and "warning". Here good states use the tertiary container and failures the error container, so they follow the wallpaper too.
