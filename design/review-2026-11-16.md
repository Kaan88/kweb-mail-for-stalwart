# Design review, 2026-11-16

Two passes on the emulator `kweb35` (Android 15 image) against the local Stalwart, signed in as `tester` with the seeded mail and a second account `second`. The first pass by the main session in light mode, wallpaper palette; the second pass by another agent, in dark mode by accident (the emulator was in night mode), so its layout findings hold and its colour remarks are dark-mode ones. Compared with `design/mockups-material.html`; the PDF could not be read on this PC. Per-theme and dark-mode comparison skipped on the owner's word. Dubious findings were checked against the code before being written down. Nothing in the app was changed.

Seen: mail list, drawer, accounts sheet, conversation (collapsed rows, inline reply, both overflow menus, pager), HTML message with hidden images, search, selection, calendar (empty) and the event editor, contacts (empty) and the contact editor, files (empty), compose, Settings top to bottom, Scheduled, Senders and signatures, Notifications, Encryption with the import and paste flows, AI assistant settings with the Service dropdown and the Models sheet, Theme dialog, Delivery check, Notification buttons, Push through Google dialog, Account security, Server administration (locked, as a UI tree), Away message, Mail filters, Manage folders, Shared with me (empty), assistant chat (empty) with its overflow.

Not seen: the event screen with a real event (the server has none), Google-mode screens (registration fails on this emulator), the layout of Server administration and Account security as pictures (both set `FLAG_SECURE`, so captures are black by design; the admin screen's text was read from the UI tree and matches the mock-up), dark mode and the other palettes as a comparison.

No app crash in logcat in either pass. The emulator process died once in the first pass, cause unknown, not the app; it came back from its snapshot in 20 s. The Mail badge, the drawer's Inbox count and the bold Inbox rows agree (2); the drop from 7 seen in the first pass was two opened threads with several unread messages each, not a bug.

## 1. Screens against the mock-ups

What matches: list rows (unread raised square, read circle, chips, swipe), drawer layout and order, the accounts sheet (current account with its count and check, the second, Add account), the conversation group with inline replies and the Reply bar, the tonal "Remote images are hidden" bar, search with highlights, compose layout, Delivery check with hero card and status pills including the failing state, Account security groups, the Notifications screen groups, the locked administration screen's text, the empty states of Calendar, Files and Shared with me. The Material You look is consistent; no screen writes its own colours.

Differences, worth a decision:

1. **Drawer: Delivery check has no state word.** The mock-up's drawer row says "Live" at the right; the app's drawer row is bare (the Delivery check screen itself has the pill). Cheap and useful: the one place a user looks for "does it work".
2. **Calendar top bar is crowded.** "Novembe…" is cut off although the row has room; the month name, two arrows, the Today pill, an overflow and the account letter share one row (`ui/calendar/CalendarScreen.kt`, `TitleRow`). The mock-up has the month as a title line with the week strip under it and Today as the only button. Move the arrows out (swipe the strip) or drop the account letter here.
3. **FAB style differs by tab.** Calendar's "+" is the solid primary (on purpose, says a comment: no agenda card is near it), Contacts' and Files' are the primary container, Mail's is an extended "Compose". One FAB style across tabs, and a label on Calendar's ("New entry") as Mail has one.
4. **Settings subtitle for AI assistant is a bare model name** ("haiku"). Say service and state: "On · Anthropic · haiku" or "Off".
5. **Account security, Connected apps:** three rows all named "kweb mail", one marked "This app". The others are old sign-ins the user cannot tell apart. The core already has `created_at` on `ConnectedApp` (`core/src/model.rs`); show the sign-in date, else "Older sign-in from this app".
6. **Thread title and list subject differ.** The list shows "Re: Release plan", the thread shows "Release plan": the thread takes the oldest subject by design (`ThreadViewModel.kt`). Fine, but the two screens disagree. Keep, or strip the prefix in the list too.
7. **Collapsed rows in a thread** show the sender in bold although read (`MessageCard.kt` line 229 is always bold), and the snippet carries the quote marker ("> Shall we ship on M…"). Gmail uses normal weight for read rows and strips quoted lines from the snippet. Small, worth doing.
8. **Contacts' empty state** "No contacts yet" has no icon; Calendar, Files and Shared with me have one in a circle. Add it.
9. **Drawer's Drafts icon** is an open envelope that reads as "read mail". Use a pencil-on-paper icon.
10. **Mock-up's Files chip** was replaced by "Attachments"; fine, keep the app.

## 2. Navigation and ease of use

- **Three bottom tabs plus a drawer plus a settings tree** is the right shape for this app. The tabs are clear.
- **Notification settings live in four places**: Settings › Notifications (Mail group), Settings › Delivery check, Settings › Notification buttons, Settings › Push through Google (This app group); the drawer has Delivery check again and Delivery check has a "Notifications" row that leads back to the first. All four rows in Settings share the same bell icon, so they look like one thing split in four. A user looking for "sound" opens two or three of these. Proposal: one "Notifications" entry in Settings holding what to notify, per address, sound (link to the channel), buttons, reminders; Delivery check stays as the diagnostic screen (drawer and Settings); "Push through Google" becomes one switch inside Delivery check (it already has the "Use push through Google instead" link). That removes two rows from Settings.
- **"Add account" sits inside the account group**, under Login and Server. It is an app-level action; the accounts sheet already has it (as the mock-up). Keep it there and at the end of Settings, not between an account's rows.
- **"Remove account" (or "Sign out" with one account) sits under "This app".** It is the account's. Move it to the end of the account group, red as now.
- **Drawer head.** Tapping the account header toggles an account list that pushes the folders down and stays expanded; after returning from Settings and from the AI settings screen the drawer came back open, still expanded. Close the drawer when a destination outside the list is opened, and collapse the account list with it. The accounts sheet from the avatar does the same job; two ways to switch accounts is one too many (section 3).
- **No theme picker in the drawer.** CLAUDE.md says the picker is in the folder drawer; it is in Settings › Theme since the Settings screen was added (PLAN.md, "Decided without asking"). CLAUDE.md is stale on this point, the app is fine.
- **Theme is a dialog of colours only** (five radio rows: wallpaper and four built-in). No light/dark/system choice and no pure black; Android handles light and dark, but every comparable app offers it. Belongs with the proposal in section 6.
- **Calendar event editor** "New entry": title, Event/Task, All day, Starts/Ends chips, Repeat, Reminder, Place, Notes; fits one screen, X returns to the calendar. No calendar picker when the account has several calendars, no guests, no colour. The first is a gap (the entry goes into which calendar?); the other two are M5's decisions, fine.
- **Contact editor**: First name, Last name, Organization, Email rows with a type chip defaulting to "Other", Add phone, Add postal address, Notes. Default the first address's type to "Home" or leave it untyped; "Other" as a default looks like a mistake.
- **Drawer entries "Ask the assistant" and "Shared with me"** are fine. "Manage folders" at the end of the folder list is good; the pencil icon reads as "edit", fine.
- **Assistant chat overflow** (New chat, Automatic changes, Assistant settings) has no icons while the thread menus have them. Give it icons or drop them in the thread menus; one rule.
- **Thread overflow**: Mark unread, Star, Ask the assistant about this, Summarize, Labels, Move to…, Report spam; message overflow: Reply, Reply all, Forward, Filter mail like this, Block sender, Labels, Summarize, Draft a reply. "Ask the assistant about this" and "Summarize" share the sparkle icon; "Summarize" and "Labels" appear in both menus. Acceptable, but a submenu "Assistant" (Ask about this, Summarize, Draft a reply) would shorten both.
- **Search back behaviour:** Back from search returns to the list; one more Back leaves the app. Correct, nothing to change.
- **Mail filters help text** points to "Manage folders in the folder list" in a sentence; a tappable link would be better than a direction.
- **Away message** has no explanation of what "From: Right away" and "Until" do until tapped; acceptable.
- **Selection** is reachable by long press and by the avatar, as in Gmail; the bar shows X, count, archive, delete, mark unread, overflow. Good. In dark mode the selected row's date, clip and snippet become very faint against the tinted card; check the on-colour of the selected card in the dark-mode pass.
- **Models sheet in the assistant settings** showed the raw core error "the service refused the key" with Retry when the local bridge was not running. The text comes from the core (`core/src/assistant/provider.rs`, for 401 and 403) and is shown as a sentence without a capital; for a keyless local address it points the wrong way. Wrap it on the Android side ("The service refused the key. Check the address and the key.") and say "could not be reached" for a connection failure.

## 3. Redundant or out of place

- Delivery check reachable from drawer and Settings: keep both, it is the diagnostic screen.
- "Notifications" row inside Delivery check duplicates Settings › Notifications: keep as a link, drop the "What to show" heading over it once section 2's merge is done.
- "Push through Google" as a Settings row and as a link in Delivery check: keep one (Delivery check).
- Two ways to switch accounts: the avatar's bottom sheet and the expanding drawer head. Keep the sheet (it is the mock-up's), make the drawer head open the same sheet.
- Icons: Account security and Server administration share the lock; the four notification rows share the bell. One icon per meaning (a key for security, a server or shield for administration; the bell only on Notifications, a check or pulse on Delivery check, a tune icon on Notification buttons, a cloud on Google push).
- "Service" appears twice on the AI assistant settings screen: the segmented control's heading SERVICE (OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic) and the dropdown label "Service" (OpenAI, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Mistral, DeepSeek, Groq, Other address). Call the first "Protocol" or fold the two into the dropdown (Anthropic as one more entry).
- The "kweb-core 0.0.1" footer on Delivery check is the only version text in the app: an About row in Settings should carry it (version, licences, source link).
- Encryption screen title "Encryption (S/MIME and OpenPGP)" runs to the right edge of the top bar and of its Settings row. "Signed and encrypted mail" as the title, the standards in the subtitle (the row's subtitle already reads "Signed and encrypted mail, and your keys").
- Encryption, "Sign new messages": the text "Switches Sign on in every new message sent from an address you have a private key for. You can switch it off per message." reads oddly. "Sign every new message from an address you have a private key for. Can be switched off per message." The key rows are text-heavy and the import buttons have no icons; a file icon on the two imports and a paste icon on the third.

## 4. The bento home proposal

Judgement: **not worth building now.** The tiles the app can honestly fill (unread, next event, delivery state, flagged and files, tasks) duplicate the first screen of each tab and the drawer's Delivery check; the tiles that would justify a home ("to pay", "trip", "people") need the assistant to read mail, and the assistant is opt-in and bring-your-own-service. An off-by-default start screen also doubles the screens to keep in each theme. Revisit if the assistant gets a daily digest; then a home with assistant tiles has a reason to exist. The one piece worth taking from it today is a "Today" strip at the top of the Calendar tab.

## 5. Android and Material 3 conventions

Meets: edge-to-edge, navigation bar with badge, search bar as top app bar, filter chips, grouped list rows, tonal bars for notices, predictive back is enabled by the target SDK, `adjustResize` fixed the keyboard, bottom sheets for accounts and models, segmented buttons for Event/Task and the assistant's protocol. Switches are one component everywhere (`ui/theme/KwebSwitch.kt`: the check on the thumb shows only when on, which is the M3 default); the first pass's "two switch styles" was off switches next to on ones, no finding.

To align:

- **Top bars** on sub-screens use the small variant with a back arrow; the mail list and tabs use a search bar. Fine. The thread screen has no title in the bar, only in the body; Gmail does the same, keep it.
- **Primary buttons** ("Add a sender", "New folder", "Save", "Send a test notification") are full-width filled pills; M3 prefers a FAB or a right-aligned button for the single action of a screen. Full width is acceptable on short screens; keep, but do not add more.
- **FAB**: a plain "+" on Calendar, Contacts and Files, an extended one on Mail. M3 allows both; the extended one with a label is the better one for a tab's main action. See section 1, item 3.
- **Empty states** use an icon in a circle and one line, consistent except Contacts. Good.
- **Dialogs**: the Theme picker is an alert dialog with radio rows; M3 would make it a bottom sheet or a screen when there are more than three options and a preview. Becomes a screen with the appearance settings proposed below.
- **Compose** has "Cc Bcc" as a text button at the right of To (`RecipientField.kt`); M3 and Gmail use a chevron. Minor.
- **Red paragraphs on Delivery check** ("Went back to the permanent connection: …", "Tap to let the app run in the background") are free paragraphs in the error colour; they read as an error for the whole screen. Put them in the row's supporting text in the error colour.
- **Menus**: icons in the thread menus, none in the chat's; one rule (section 2).

## 6. Settings: what a user expects

Today the app stores, per account: notification on/off, remove when read elsewhere, reminders, per-address notify choice (Notify, Quiet, Do not notify), folders that notify, notification buttons, Google mode, assistant service, model and consents, encryption on/off and "sign new messages", senders and signatures, away message, filters; and for the app: the theme colour (`ui/UiPrefs.kt` holds only the theme, the translate language and whether the notification permission was asked). Swipes are archive and delete and fixed (`ui/list/MailListScreen.kt`), the Undo time is 10 s and fixed (`core/src/compose/flush.rs`), a conversation is marked read when the pager settles on it, with no choice. ## 7. Proposed changes, ordered by how much they matter

1. Sound per receiving address (channel per address) and preview text choice. Core change: a channel id per address; the Notifications screen gains a "Sound" row per address that opens the channel.
2. Always show pictures: global switch under Reading plus "Always from this sender" on the Show button, with the allow list in the core (per account, never for shared accounts).
3. Merge the notification entries in Settings into one (section 2); move Add account and Remove account; one icon per row meaning.
4. Appearance screen: colour, light/dark/system, pure black; replaces the Theme dialog.
5. Swipe actions configurable; confirm before delete; undo-send time.
6. Reading settings: conversation view, mark read on open, auto-advance, text size, preview lines.
7. About row with version (fixes "nothing shows the version"); per-app language entry in the manifest.
8. Calendar: month as a title, fewer buttons in the row, a labelled FAB; one FAB style across tabs; a calendar picker in the editor when there are several.
9. Drawer: Delivery check state word; the head opens the accounts sheet instead of expanding; the drawer closes when a destination outside the list is opened; Drafts icon.
10. Texts: AI assistant subtitle; Connected apps rows with their date; the Models sheet error; the Encryption title and the "Sign new messages" text; "Service" once on the assistant screen.
11. Thread: read collapsed rows in normal weight, quote markers out of the snippet; Contacts' empty-state icon; icons in the chat's overflow; red paragraphs in Delivery check into row text; "Cc Bcc" chevron.
12. Sync window and clear cache rows; account name per account.
13. Bento home: not now (section 4).

Nothing above is started. The owner picks.
