A native Android client for Stalwart 1.1 and later: mail, calendar, contacts and files in one app. It speaks JMAP only (no IMAP, POP3 or SMTP).

<b>Mail</b>: conversations, folders and labels, search, send with Undo and send later, drafts, filters, signed and encrypted mail (OpenPGP and S/MIME, off until you turn it on).

<b>Notifications that stay true</b>: new mail shows within seconds and goes away when you read, archive or delete it anywhere else. Delivered through a permanent connection to your own server, or optionally through Google push with no relay in between.

<b>Calendar, tasks, contacts and files</b>, shared folders and calendars, and an optional mirror of your calendars into the phone's calendar.

<b>AI assistant (optional, bring your own)</b>: off until you enter a service and agree to it. Mail is only sent to the service you named, for a task you started.

By itself the app only talks to the server you sign in to. Everything else is optional and starts with you: pictures in a mail, the AI service you entered, a key lookup you ask for, Google push.
